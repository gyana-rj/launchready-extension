LaunchReady privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

No data leaves your browser. LaunchReady has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What LaunchReady does

LaunchReady checks a web page for common launch mistakes (such as a noindex tag, a staging address in the canonical link, or a missing title) and explains how to fix them. Every check runs inside your browser.

What it reads

  • The page you ask it to scan: its HTML, and the response headers, robots.txt and same-site links of that page.
  • For a bulk scan (Pro), the web addresses you paste in, fetched by your browser directly from those sites.

These requests go from your browser to the website being checked, just like visiting it. They never go to us or to any third party. LaunchReady only scans when you click Scan or Start bulk scan.

What it stores, and where

The following is saved on your device only, in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never sent anywhere.

  • How many free scans you have used today.
  • Your most recent scan result.
  • Your audit history (up to 200 audits) and your report branding (agency name, client name, date and logo).

You can delete the history on the History page and remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data or browsing history.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • It does not load or run code from the internet. All code ships inside the extension.
  • It does not scan pages in the background or on its own.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to run the checks on the page you chose to scan, when you click the extension.
  • storage: to keep the local data listed above.
  • Optional site access: only requested when you run a bulk scan, so your browser can fetch the pages you pasted. You can revoke it at any time in chrome://extensions.

Beta, licensing and payments

LaunchReady is in a free beta: every feature is included, there are no accounts, no payments and no license keys. If paid plans are added later, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and it will say exactly what is sent and to whom.
